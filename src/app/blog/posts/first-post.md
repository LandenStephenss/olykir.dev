For a while, **olykir.dev** has been a place to showcase projects and ideas, but it always felt like something was missing. There was nowhere to share the process behind building these projects, the mistakes, the lessons, and everything that happens between the first idea and the end result.

That is what this blog is for. A portfolio can show what I made, but it can't always explain why I made it that way, what changed along the way, or what I learned when the first attempt didn't work. I wanted a place for those details, too.

## Building in Public

I don't want every project update to read like a polished case study. A lot of development is figuring things out as you go: trying an approach, finding its limits, and deciding what to do next. Writing about that process makes the work feel more honest, and it gives me a useful record to return to later.

Some posts might focus on a technical problem or a design decision. Others may be simple progress updates about something I'm building. The goal isn't to pretend every experiment turns into a finished product; it's to share the thinking that goes into making one.

## A mix of interests

My work moves between web development, firmware, and hardware. Those areas come with different constraints, but they often meet in the same project. I want this blog to make room for that range, from the software a person interacts with to the embedded systems that make a device work.

I'll also share the occasional thought from outside the code editor. Cars and automotive culture are a big part of what I enjoy, and they influence the things I build and the content I make. This site is a better home for those longer thoughts than a quick social post.

## Why write it down?

Putting an idea into words forces me to slow down and understand it. It helps me notice what went well, what I'd change next time, and which details are worth carrying into the next project. If someone else finds a useful idea here, even better.

This is a starting point, not a promise to publish on a strict schedule. I'll add to the blog as projects develop and there is something worth sharing. Thanks for reading, and I hope you'll follow along.
